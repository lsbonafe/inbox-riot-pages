# Editorial contract

Use the connected mail labels named in the private instance's scheduled prompt as input. Do not classify, archive, move, mark read, or remove any source label. Deduplicate messages across child labels and across published issues using canonical source URLs or email-only publication references. A processed label is a checkpoint only after successful publication or a completed no-issue report. Treat account, finance, health, travel, orders, housing, personal, and administrative mail as private by default. Include only clearly public editorial material with an independent public source; never publish personal transactions, credentials, medical details, addresses, or correspondence.

Read every substantive candidate. For Reads, open the full original article when available and use the email body when the work is email-only. Keep each Read's headline, takeaways, relevance, and excerpt in the original work's language. Give non-English Reads a short original-language badge through `sourceLanguage`. Cite the canonical public URL; for email-only work, use a human-readable publication/date reference without mailbox IDs or private links. Explain what the source says, why it matters, and any real uncertainty. Distinguish evidence from editorial inference.

Rank lead topics by consequence, novelty, relevance to the configured editor's interests, and source strength. Each lead needs a substantial takeaway as well as claim, relevance, uncertainty, and supporting stories. Use Reading Room for the substance of essays and articles; Signal Desk for changes and announcements; New Releases for specific things released; Offers for public offers worth evaluating; Noise Floor for noteworthy patterns of hype. Inclusion is an editorial choice, not a mirror of email volume.

Apply `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.json`, and `ART_DIRECTION.md` to the landing page, issues, and generated art. Use article-specific art, varied palettes and templates, and relevant source images when rights permit. Keep handwritten type to short static notes. Do not label original art on the page. Source-hosted images require direct public HTTPS URLs, credit, and a link to the source. Use brief attributed excerpts; never reproduce a whole newsletter.

Store only edited public-safe issue JSON and approved assets in the private instance. Never put raw email, Gmail IDs, subscriber links, tokens, payment data, or private drafts in the public engine or `gh-pages`. Run tests, Astro check, build, and artifact privacy validation before a release. The private repository's workflow alone publishes `dist/` to `gh-pages`; the public `main` branch is reusable source.
